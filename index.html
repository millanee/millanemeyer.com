About

Software that has to hold up.

Millane Meyer is a software engineer with an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Copenhagen and a B.Sc. in Software Engineering from Ulm University. As a working student at ZEISS SMT and Syntegon Technology, she built software that had to survive a pipeline, a field study, or a normal working day.

At ZEISS she automated UI tests with Playwright and C# and wired them into Azure DevOps. At Syntegon she built a Visual Basic monitoring interface that made system and synchronisation state visible to people in more than 50 teams.

Her master's thesis, graded 12 — the top mark on the Danish scale — is an Android app for context-sensitive interventions around social media use. A 7-day field study with 14 participants produced 595 data points, analysed in Python.
